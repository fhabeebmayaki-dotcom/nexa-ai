Security & Privacy

What this prototype actually does: everything runs in your browser. Demo sign-in accepts any email/password locally and creates no account. CSV uploads are parsed in your browser and never leave it. There is no database, no server-side AI, and no third-party data processing in this prototype — so no security claim is made about a production system.

What a production NEXA would need (not yet built): managed authentication, row-level access control, server-side secrets for any AI provider, upload size/format limits, retention and deletion controls, and audit logging. See SETUP.md.

Data minimisation in the demo: demo authors are anonymised IDs (e.g. user_014), not real people. Do not paste real private customer data into this prototype — it has no access controls.

Audience Signals — emerging topics

Method: compare the most recent 14 days against the prior 14 days in the current filtered dataset. A topic is called “emerging” only when it has at least 5 records in the recent window and its recent count is at least 1.5× the prior window (prior window of 0 counts only if recent ≥ 8). Low-volume topics are marked “insufficient data” and are not called emerging.
